# MPP — Media Process Platform (V5.0)

MPP (Media Process Platform) is Hi3403V100's unified framework that
ties together ISP, video input/output/processing, codecs, audio,
region management, geometric distortion correction, and the video
graphics subsystem. Below the chapters of the vendor *MPP development
reference* are split out for chapter-by-chapter browsing.
